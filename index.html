How to prepare a yam porage

Yam porage is a special type of meal which is common in West Africa.

Brief Intro

What is a Yam

A Yam is a starchy vegetable that grows underground. Yams are similar to potatoes; they can be baked, smashed, boiled, grated etc. Porage is just one of the meals you can cook with yam. You can use yam to prepare pounded yam, you can fry it, and you can also just boil it.

Yam Porage Preparation Requirements

Before we start, these are the basic requirements:

  • Yam
  • Water
  • Oil
  • Ingredients (such as:)
    • Pepper
    • Salt
    • Your favourite seasoning
    • Onion

Cooking Time

Let's assume you have already decided how many people are going to eat the food. This is the most important thing to consider any time you are cooking.

Now that we have the basic requirements, it is time to start cooking.

  1. Slice the yam: Make sure all the back are removed.
  2. Chop the yam into small pieces.
  3. Add fresh water to the yam and wash it.
  4. Prepare a cooking pot, make sure it is clean.
  5. Put the cooking pot on heat: meaning put the pot on a fire, electric cooker or any type of heat source available to you and immediately add some water.
  6. Gradually begin adding the yam.
  7. Cover it and allow it to cook.
  8. Depending on your heat source, it should cook for 30 minutes to 1 hour.
  9. Now add some salt.
  10. Add pepper.
  11. Add seasoning.
  12. Add oil.
  13. Use a spoon and begin turning the yam. Make sure everything is mixed.
  14. Allow it to cook for about 10 to 15 minutes.
  15. Use a spoon and start smarshing the yam.
  16. After smarshing, leave it for about 5 minutes.
  17. Finally, yam porage is done!

Safety Notes

Please be careful when putting the pot on the fire. Never touch a hot pot with your bare hands. Also, watch out for steam when you open the lid, as it can burn your face.

Cooking Terms

Boil
To cook food in water that is heated to 100 degrees Celsius (bubbling).
smarsh
To press the food with a spoon or fork until it becomes soft and mashed.
Simmer
To cook food gently on a low heat so it bubbles slightly.
